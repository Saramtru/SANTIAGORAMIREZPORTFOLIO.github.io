Master's thesis · 2026 · Joint Research Centre (JRC)

Master/slave teleoperator for nuclear hot cells

Automation of a pulley-driven teleoperator arm that lets operators handle material inside shielded nuclear cells without being exposed.

  • General modelling pipeline for pulley transmissions, with parameters identified from experimental measurements instead of complex analytical models.
  • Inverse kinematics formulated and solved as an Optimal Control Problem, with signal reconstruction and post-processing for the motion profiles.
  • CAD design of the motor mounts, verified with FEM, and of 3D-printed sensor mounts for the test bench.
  • Integrated control architecture and a digital twin of the teleoperator.

Structural dynamics · Oil & gas

Dynamic FEA of a conveyor-belt support structure

Modal analysis of the steel structure that supports a coal-transport conveyor, to prevent failures caused by resonance.

  • Finite element model of the support truss, with its beams, nodes and three ground supports.
  • Computation of the natural frequencies and mode shapes of the structure.
  • Check of the eigenfrequencies against the dynamic loads of the conveyor, to keep the structure out of resonance.

Project
Data analysis · Smart materials

Eigenfrequency identification of a Nitinol SMA damper

How the natural frequency of an adaptive tuned mass damper with shape-memory-alloy beams changes with temperature.

  • Vibration tests on a shaker, measuring the response of the damper with an impedance head.
  • Controlled heating of the Nitinol beams with electric current to change their temperature.
  • Identification of the natural frequencies from the measured frequency response at each temperature.
  • Comparison of the experimental results with a theoretical model of the damper.
